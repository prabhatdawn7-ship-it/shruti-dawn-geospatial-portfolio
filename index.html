Current Thesis

Research direction combining deep learning, remote sensing and environmental interaction analysis in hilly and rural landscapes.

Environmentally-Aware GeoAI Framework for Automated Rural Road Detection and Environmental Interaction Analysis in the Hilly and Rural Landscapes of Maval Taluka and Mahabaleshwar Using Deep Learning and Remote Sensing

Study focus: rural road detection/segmentation and the relationship between road networks, terrain, vegetation, drainage, land use and landscape structure.
